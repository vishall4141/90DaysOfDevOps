# My 90-Day DevOps & Cloud Engineering Career Execution Blueprint

## 1. What Is My Understanding of DevOps and Cloud Engineering?

My understanding is that Cloud Engineering focuses on building, configuring, securing, and maintaining the infrastructure required to run applications reliably in the cloud. It involves services such as compute, networking, storage, databases, IAM, monitoring, and cost optimization.

DevOps is about connecting development and operations through automation, collaboration, continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD), infrastructure as code, monitoring, and reliable deployment practices.

I see Cloud Engineering and DevOps as closely connected. Cloud Engineering provides the infrastructure, while DevOps helps automate how applications are built, tested, deployed, monitored, and maintained.

For me, the goal is not just to make an application work. It is to understand how to build and manage infrastructure that is secure, reliable, scalable, automated, and cost-effective in a real production environment.

## 2. Why Am I Starting My DevOps and Cloud Learning Journey?

I have already started gaining practical experience as a Cloud Engineer Intern at AvQuint Innovations. Through my work, I have explored AWS services, configured infrastructure, worked on VPCs and security groups, managed IAM permissions, connected applications with databases, and learned about monitoring, networking, and cloud cost management.

I have also worked on cloud-based application deployments and collaborated with developers and other team members on infrastructure-related tasks.

These experiences have helped me understand the importance of cloud infrastructure, but they have also shown me that there is much more to learn, especially in automation, CI/CD pipelines, Terraform, Linux, Docker, Kubernetes, and production-level operations.

I am starting this 90-day journey to strengthen my fundamentals, improve my practical skills, fill my knowledge gaps, and become more confident in solving real-world engineering problems.

My goal is to move beyond configuring individual AWS services and learn how to connect the entire workflow, from code to deployment, monitoring, troubleshooting, and maintenance.

## 3. Where Do I Want to Reach in 90 Days?

By the end of these 90 days, I want to become a more capable Cloud and DevOps Engineer who can independently build, deploy, troubleshoot, and maintain a cloud-based application using industry-standard tools and practices.

I want to develop practical skills in:

- **Linux and scripting:** Become comfortable with Linux administration, shell commands, permissions, processes, logs, and Bash scripting.
- **Networking and AWS:** Strengthen my understanding of VPCs, subnets, routing, security groups, IAM, load balancing, databases, and cloud security.
- **Git and GitHub:** Manage source code, branches, commits, and collaborative development workflows.
- **Docker:** Containerize applications and understand images, containers, networking, volumes, and deployment.
- **CI/CD:** Build automated pipelines to test, build, and deploy applications.
- **Terraform:** Provision and manage cloud infrastructure using reusable infrastructure-as-code modules.
- **Monitoring and troubleshooting:** Use logs, metrics, alerts, and debugging techniques to identify and resolve issues.
- **Kubernetes:** Build a foundation in container orchestration after becoming comfortable with Docker and deployment fundamentals.

My target is to complete at least one end-to-end, production-style portfolio project that demonstrates these skills. I also want to document my work, explain my architectural decisions, understand the costs and security implications, and be able to discuss my implementation confidently in technical interviews.

## 4. How Will I Stay Consistent Every Day?

I understand that learning DevOps is not about watching tutorials or collecting certificates. It requires regular hands-on practice, troubleshooting, and building things myself.

To stay consistent, I will follow these principles throughout the next 90 days:

1. **Daily learning:** Dedicate at least 60–90 minutes each day to learning and practical implementation, adjusting the duration when work commitments require it.
2. **Hands-on practice:** For every major concept, perform a practical task instead of relying only on theory.
3. **Learn one step at a time:** Understand the fundamentals before moving to advanced tools. Avoid rushing through multiple technologies without being able to use them.
4. **Document my progress:** Maintain notes of what I learned, commands I used, problems I faced, and how I solved them.
5. **Build continuously:** Apply each new skill to a real project instead of treating every topic as an isolated exercise.
6. **Review weekly:** Evaluate what I completed, identify weak areas, and plan the next week's tasks.
7. **Share my learning:** Where appropriate, publish project updates, architecture diagrams, or technical lessons on GitHub and AWS Builder Center.
8. **Focus on consistency over perfection:** If I have a busy day, I will complete a smaller task rather than abandon the routine entirely.

## My Commitment

For the next 90 days, I will treat learning DevOps and Cloud Engineering as a career priority. I will focus on practical skills, consistent execution, and measurable progress rather than simply completing tutorials.

I know that 90 days will not make me an expert in every tool, but it can build a strong foundation and help me become more independent, technically confident, and prepared for greater responsibilities as a Cloud and DevOps Engineer.

**My goal is to become someone who can not only understand cloud infrastructure but also automate it, deploy applications reliably, troubleshoot problems, and explain why a solution is designed the way it is.**
